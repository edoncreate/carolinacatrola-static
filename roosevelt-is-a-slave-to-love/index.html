Roosevelt is a Slave to Love

The 80’s vibe is very much present in all I do. I love the synthwaves, the retro colors, the design and the 90’s horizon in front of us. There is a musician that go by the name of Roosevelt. He creates this type of atmospheric music that just seems to take us on a trip to outer space.

Inspired by that, I developed these comic book strip series where he meets other great musicians in random daily activities.
